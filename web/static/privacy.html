Autonomous AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, Autonomous AI may receive your Google account name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier necessary to authenticate your account.

The service may store conversations, user-configured memories, settings, and files needed to provide requested features.

How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users, provide the AI assistant, maintain conversation history, and provide personalized features such as long-term memory.

Google data

Google account information is used for account authentication and related functionality. It is not sold.

Optional Gmail features

Gmail-related features are optional and require separate authorization. The service only uses the permissions granted for those features.

Conversations and memory

Conversation history and saved memory may be retained to provide continuity and personalization. Available controls allow users to delete conversations and memories.

Third-party services

Autonomous AI may use services such as Google, Groq, cloud hosting providers, and database providers.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, although no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support address listed for the application.
